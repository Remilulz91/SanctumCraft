## [0.8.0-alpha.3] - Patchouli guide

### Changed
- **The Sanctum Guide is now a Patchouli book** (Patchouli is now required, client and server). 6 categories and 19 entries: real crafting grids, a 3D portal preview, item spotlights, search, links between entries. The text follows the game language (French or English). Still given once on first join (everyone gets the new book once), `/sanctum guide` for a new one, and in the creative tab. ### Removed

## [0.8.0-alpha.2] - Guide fixes

### Changed
- **`/sanctum leave` removed**: the only ways out are the arrival portal and the exit portal opened by the destination mechanism. Manage your time.
- Guide: one recipe per page with the full legend for every letter, readable colors for the keys (no more white text on the page), and an end page closing each language with a link to the other language and to the cover.

## [0.8.0-alpha.1] - Guide book, welcome message, update check

### Added
- **Sanctum Guide**: a book given once on first join, with a French part and an English part. The cover and the clickable contents jump straight to each chapter (portal, recipes, raid, difficulties, eggs, chests and keys, monsters and traps, Altar, talents, relics and showcase, incubator, companions, rolling egg and HUD, commands, tips); every page links back to the contents. Lost it? `/sanctum guide` (everyone) gives a new one. Books in the inventory are updated to the installed version's text on join and when opened. Text in `src/main/resources/sanctumcraft/guide.txt`, layout checked by `tools/check_guide.py`.
- **Welcome message** on joining a world: mod name, version, author, short description, clickable `/sanctum guide` and GitHub link (`welcomeMessage`).
- **Update check**: looks at the GitHub releases (pre-releases included) once at startup, in the background; if a newer version exists, the welcome message says so with a download link, and the server log too (`updateCheck`).

### Changed
- Unsecured eggs can't go in the incubator anymore, even in creative mode. Secured eggs (creative tab or `/sanctum egg`) still can.

## [0.7.0-alpha.2] - Fixes

### Fixed
- **Free arrows from traps**: a trap dispenser overwritten during generation spilled its 16 arrows on the floor. Items spilled while a sanctum is being generated or cleared are now removed, and arrows shot by trap dispensers can no longer be picked up.

## [0.7.0-alpha.1] - HUD, config screen, rolling egg

### Added
- **Sanctum HUD** (top left, client): difficulty and timer, next reinforcement wave, unsecured eggs carried, team (health / dead / out), active companion (health or KO time), rolling egg dash, Shells and Hunter level. Sent by the server once per second and counted down locally. **H** toggles it; `hudEnabled` / `hudScalePercent` options.
- **Config screen**: every option editable in game through Mod Menu when Cloth Config is installed (both optional, not bundled). `SanctumConfig.save()` is now public.
- **Rolling egg** (**R**, inside a Sanctum only): the player is drawn as a large rolling egg (body, armor, held items and name hidden; no hand in first person), +50 % speed, sneak = dash (3 s cooldown). No attacking, digging, opening or using while rolling. Taking damage, dying, leaving the Sanctum or pressing R again stands the player back up. Not allowed while carrying an unsecured egg (`rollingWithEggs`).
- Options `rollingSpeedPercent`, `rollingDashCooldownSeconds`, `rollingWithEggs`, `hudEnabled`, `hudScalePercent`.

## [0.6.1-alpha.1] - Companion collection, two-level showcase, egg ownership

### Changed
- **Companions are now a collection** (Altar > Companions, 10 slots, `companionSlots`): hatching adds the companion to the collection instead of spawning a pet. One companion is active.
  - **Companion Whistle** (given at the first hatching, craft: gold ingot + amethyst shard + string): right-click to summon the active companion next to you, again to call it back.
  - The summoned companion **follows you through every dimension change** (sanctum portals included): no more pushing pets through the portal.
  - A defeated companion is **knocked out**, not lost: back after 3 / 4 / 6 / 8 min (common → legendary). No drops.
  - **Species**: Wolf (fighter), Fox (fast, fighter), Allay (support, flies), Iron Golem (tank, epic and legendary eggs only).
  - **Random stats** at every hatching: health, damage and speed rolled in a range per rarity (common −20 %…+20 %, legendary +10 %…+60 %); traits have a random power (50–150 %). Two common wolves can be very different: keep the best one.
  - **Release** a companion (right-click twice in the menu) for Shells and Hunter XP.
  - Wolves and cats hatched in 0.6.0 stay as normal tamed pets.
- **Showcase with two levels per relic**: display the **classic** version first (+1 % Shells), then the **collector** version on top (+3 % more). Row bonuses: 4 classics (+1 heart / +2 armor / +10 % Shells), 4 collectors (+2 hearts / +4 armor / +25 % Shells). Collector relics placed in 0.6.0 count for both levels.
- **Relics can drop from every chest**, by chest rarity only (difficulty only changes the number of items): 1 / 2 / 4 / 8 / 15 % (basic → legendary), with 3 / 5 / 8 / 12 / 18 % of collector versions.
- **Eggs belong to the player who extracts them** (owner shown in the tooltip). Anyone can incubate an egg, but the companion always joins the **owner's** collection, even if the owner is offline.

### Fixed
- **Incubator**: it now switches to "ready" by itself as soon as the time is up (green top, sound, particles, message to the owner), and **one click** hatches the egg.

## [0.6.0-alpha.1] - Talents, incubator, companions and showcase

### Added
- **Talents** (Altar > Talents): 1 point per Hunter level, 3 branches × 4 talents × 3 ranks. Bonuses apply inside the sanctum only.
  - Survival: Vitality (+2 hearts/rank), Toughness (+2 armor/rank), Fleet Foot (+5 % speed/rank), Second Wind (regeneration below 30 % health, once a minute).
  - Exploration: Quick Hands (-15 % chest search/rank), Digger (-2 nest hits/rank), Strong Back (-1 egg slowness level/rank), Night Eyes (night vision; rank 2+: direction of the nearest active nest every 30 s).
  - Loot: Greed (+10 % Shells/rank), Keysmith (+5 % key chance/rank), Treasure Hunter (10 %/rank extra loot roll), Collector (+3 % collector relic chance/rank).
  - Reset for 300 × level Shells.
- **Incubator** (craft: amethyst shard / glass / amethyst shard, glass / hay bale / glass, 3 Rune Stones): put a **secured** egg in it; it hatches after 10 / 20 / 40 / 60 min of game time (common → legendary), even if the player is offline (not while the server is off). Breaking it gives the egg back.
- **Companions**: hatching gives a tamed wolf (75 %) or cat (25 %), with health/damage ×1 / ×1.3 / ×1.7 / ×2.2 by rarity, a wolf coat by rarity, and 1 / 1 / 2 / 3 traits: Ferocious (+25 % damage), Sturdy (+50 % health), Swift (+20 % speed), Healer (heals its owner 1 heart / 10 s), Tracker (points to the nearest active nest every 20 s), Guardian (owner gets Resistance I). Healer / Tracker / Guardian work in the sanctum.
- **Companions travel with you**: a companion standing (not sitting) within 10 blocks follows you into the sanctum and back out (`maxCompanionsInSanctum`, default 1). A companion left behind is lost when the sanctum collapses.
- **Relics**: 12 relics in 3 collections (Ruins, Depths, Nest), found in epic (8 %) and legendary (20 %) chests; 10 % of them are **collector** versions (glinting, gold name). Normal relics sell for 150 Shells; collector relics can't be sold.
- **Showcase** (Altar > Showcase): display collector relics permanently. Each relic: +2 % Shells. Full rows: Ruins +2 hearts, Depths +4 armor (in the sanctum), Nest +25 % Shells.
- Config: `incubationTimePercent` (100), `maxCompanionsInSanctum` (1).

## [0.5.0-alpha.1] - Altar, Shells, Hunter levels and shop

### Added
- **Sanctum Altar** (craft: Sanctum Shard on top, Rune Stone / Lodestone / Rune Stone, 3 Rune Stones): opens the altar menu (home, shop, sell, profile).
- **Shells**: per-player currency (a balance, not an item: it can't be lost). Earned by extracting eggs (50 / 120 / 300 / 800 per common / rare / epic / legendary egg × difficulty loot multiplier) and by selling loot.

- **Shop**: keys (600 / 1500 / 3500), Sanctum Shard (400), 8 Rune Stones (200), healing and strength potions, golden apple, arrows, torches, food. **Chaos Ticket**: 2500 Shells, ×1.5 for every ticket bought the same day, reset at midnight (Europe/Paris by default).
- **Sell screen**: put items in the top area, the bottom row shows the total; confirm to sell. Unsellable items are given back when the screen closes. **Eggs can't be sold.**
- **Profile**: level, XP bar, Shells, raids, extractions, eggs (altar or `/sanctum profile`).
- Debug: `/sanctum debug shells <amount>`, `/sanctum debug hunterxp <amount>`.
- Config: `chaosTicketPrice` (2500), `chaosTicketPriceMultiplier` (1.5), `dailyResetZone` (Europe/Paris).

## [0.4.0-alpha.1] - Monsters, reinforcements, rifts and traps

### Added
- **Sanctum monsters**: vanilla mobs named "<mob> of the Sanctum", persistent, removed with the sanctum. Pool per difficulty: zombies, skeletons, spiders, husks, cave spiders; + strays, witches, vindicators in Nightmare; + wither skeletons and evokers in Chaos. Health ×1 / ×1.75 / ×2.75 and damage ×1 / ×1.5 / ×2.25 (Normal / Nightmare / Chaos); skeleton bows get Power in harder modes.
- **Initial population** when the sanctum opens: packs in about 60 % of the rooms (2 possible packs in great halls), sometimes a lone mob in corridors, none in the entrance.
- **Fixed guards**: in front of every vault door, in every nest room (active or not, so they don't give the egg away) and around the destination mechanism.
- **Reinforcement waves**, timed like the original mode (relative to the end, so they work with any duration):
  - Nightmare / Chaos: early reinforcements at 30 % of the time
  - 5:00 left: reinforcements + monster damage +25 %
  - 2:00 left: reinforcements + monster damage +50 %
  Each wave is announced with a title and a raid horn; the bonus applies to monsters already in the sanctum.
- **Rifts** (2 per sanctum, in halls): right-click to open it, survive 3 waves of monsters (every 10 s), and the rift turns into a chest (Rare or better).
- **Arrow traps** in corridors (25 % per corridor arm): pressure plates across the corridor, a dispenser in each wall.
- Config: `mobDensityPercent` (100), `maxMobsPerSanctum` (90), `riftsPerSanctum` (2), `trapChancePercent` (25).

## [0.3.0-alpha.2] - Balancing and portal safety

- **Less loot in Nightmare / Chaos**: loot multiplier 1.75 → 1.5 (Nightmare) and 2.75 → 2.0 (Chaos); a chest holds 14 items max.

## [0.3.0-alpha.1] - Chests, keys, vaults and ruins

### Added
- **Sanctum chests in 5 rarities**, each with its own color: Basic (gray), Fine (green), Rare (blue), Epic (purple), Legendary (gold). Rooms hold chest spots; about 30 % stay empty. Rarer chests are more frequent in Nightmare and Chaos, and in great halls.
- **Progressive reveal**: opening a chest shows hidden slots; items appear one by one while someone keeps the chest open (rarer items take longer), with a sound and a search bar. Hidden slots can't be clicked. The lid stays open once searched.
- **Loot tables** per rarity (`data/sanctumcraft/loot_table/chests/`). Difficulty adds extra rolls (Nightmare ×1.75, Chaos ×2.75 on average). Epic and Legendary loot can include Sanctum Shards; Legendary can include a Chaos Ticket.
- **Keys**: Rusted Iron (Normal), Silver-Plated (Nightmare), Gilded (Chaos). Found in chests (chance grows with chest rarity). Keys survive outside the sanctum and can be used in a later run.
- **Vault rooms**: 2 locked dead ends per sanctum (`vaultRooms`), closed by a locked door matching the run's difficulty. Use the key on the door to open it. Vault chests are 2 rarities higher.
- **Sanctum ruins in the Overworld**: rare natural structure (plains, forests, taiga, jungle, desert, savanna, badlands…) with a complete, unlit Rune Stone frame and a chest guaranteeing 1-2 Sanctum Shards. Find one with `/locate structure sanctumcraft:sanctum_ruins`.
- New room template `vault`; chest spots added to every room template (block `sanctumcraft:loot_spot`).

### Known limitations
- A chest opened before a server restart comes back empty after the restart (contents are not saved; the sanctum only lives 30 minutes).

## [0.2.0-alpha.1] - The raid loop: nests, eggs, extraction

### Added
- **Rooms of different sizes**: 2x2 **great halls** (50×50, 14 high), **corridors** built toward their doors only, **small rooms** at dead ends. 18 rooms per sanctum by default on a 10×10 grid.
- **New room templates**: `small_shrine`, `small_storage`, `great_hall_columns`, `great_hall_crypt`.
- **Nest candidates**: 5 nest rooms per sanctum, only 1 to 3 of them hold an egg (configurable). You never know in advance which ones.
- **Sanctum Nest** block: hit an active nest 10 times to dig its egg out. Everyone inside is told who found what.
- **Sanctum Eggs** in 4 rarities (common, rare, epic, legendary). Rarer eggs are more likely in Nightmare and Chaos.
- **Egg weight**: carrying eggs inside the sanctum slows you down (more eggs / rarer eggs = slower).
- **Eggs are always lost on death in the sanctum**, even with `keepInventory` or `sanctumKeepInventory`: they fall on the ground where teammates can pick them up.
- **Destination Mechanism** in the farthest room: right-click and stay close for 5 s to open a second exit portal.
- **Extraction**: leaving the sanctum alive (any exit portal or `/sanctum leave`) secures your eggs and grants XP (by rarity × difficulty). Secured eggs show it in their tooltip. Hatching comes in 0.6.
- Ender chests can't be used or placed in the sanctum (no stashing eggs).
- Config: `nestCandidates` (5), `activeNestsMin` (1), `activeNestsMax` (3), `nestHits` (10), `destinationActivationSeconds` (5). `roomsPerSanctum` now defaults to 18; `nestRooms` is replaced by `nestCandidates`.

## [0.1.0-alpha.1] - Foundations: dimension, portal, sanctum lifecycle

### Added
- **Sanctum dimension** (`sanctumcraft:sanctum`): empty void with the End sky, no natural mob spawns, beds and respawn anchors disabled.
- **Rune Stone** block (8 per craft: 8 deepslate bricks + 1 amethyst shard) and **Sanctum Shard** item (4 amethyst shards + 1 ender pearl).
- **Sanctum portal**: build a Rune Stone rectangle (interior 2×3 to 21×21, corners optional) and right-click it with a Sanctum Shard. The shard is consumed. Breaking the frame breaks the portal.
- **Independent portals**: every portal has its own sanctum, timer and cooldown, saved with the world.
- **Sanctum lifecycle**:
  - entering a ready portal generates a new sanctum room by room while the player waits ("Generating… Room x / y")
  - up to 3 players (configurable) can join the same sanctum through the same portal, during generation or while it is open
  - 30-minute timer shown in a boss bar, warnings at 5 min, 1 min and a 10-second countdown
  - leave at any time through the exit portal of the entrance room (or `/sanctum leave`), come back while the timer runs
  - at zero, players still inside die and the sanctum is wiped over several ticks
  - the portal then recharges (30 min by default)
- **Room assembler**: 12 rooms on an 8×8 grid of 25×25 cells: random tree from the entrance + optional loops; destination = farthest room, nests = next far rooms; unused doorways are sealed.
- **6 placeholder rooms** (entrance, nest, destination, 3 halls), generated by `tools/gen_rooms.py`.
- **Protection**: survival players can't break or place blocks in the sanctum.
- **Safety**: sanctums still generating at shutdown are discarded; players reconnecting inside a collapsed sanctum die.
- Commands: `/sanctum status`, `/sanctum leave`, `/sanctum list` (OP), `/sanctum debug generate|close|closeall|time <s>|cooldown reset` (OP, debug build).
- Config file `config/sanctumcraft.properties`.
- FR / EN translations.
