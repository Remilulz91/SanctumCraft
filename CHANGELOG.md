# Changelog

## [0.1.0-alpha.5] - Difficulties and sanctum-only keep inventory

### Added
- **Difficulty menu**: entering a free portal opens a menu (chest style): **Normal** (30 min), **Nightmare** (25 min), **Chaos** (20 min). The difficulty is shown in the boss bar, the entry title and `/sanctum status`. Mob and loot multipliers are already defined per difficulty and will be applied when mobs (0.4) and loot (0.3) arrive.
- **Chaos Ticket** item: required (and consumed) to open a Chaos sanctum. For now only obtainable in creative / with commands; it will be earned by leveling up or bought in the shop (0.5).
- **`sanctumKeepInventory`** option (default `false`): players who die inside a sanctum keep their inventory and XP, without changing the `keepInventory` gamerule for the rest of the world.
- `/sanctum debug generate [normal|nightmare|chaos]`.
- Config: `raidDurationNightmareSeconds` (1500), `raidDurationChaosSeconds` (1200), `sanctumKeepInventory` (false).

## [0.1.0-alpha.4] - No explosives in the Sanctum

### Changed
- Also forbidden inside the Sanctum: TNT, TNT minecarts, beds, respawn anchors and creeper spawn eggs.
- Creepers are removed as soon as they appear in the Sanctum (spawners, commands, anything).

## [0.1.0-alpha.3] - Survival lock and block rules

### Added
- **Survival is forced inside the Sanctum**: entering switches the player to survival, and any game mode change (`/gamemode`, F3+F4…) is refused while inside. Disabled in the development environment (`gradlew runClient`) so the mod can be debugged in creative. Config: `forceSurvival` (default `true`).
- **Placing blocks is now allowed** in the Sanctum. Blocks placed by players can be broken again; the Sanctum's own blocks stay unbreakable.
- **Explosions** (TNT, beds, later creepers) only destroy player-placed blocks inside the Sanctum.

### Changed
- Still forbidden inside the Sanctum: buckets (fluids would wash the rooms), pistons (they would move the walls), hoe/shovel/axe conversions on Sanctum blocks.

## [0.1.0-alpha.2] - Fixes

### Fixed
- **Recipes missing from the recipe book**: Rune Stone and Sanctum Shard recipes are now unlocked as soon as you pick up an amethyst shard (or deepslate bricks / an ender pearl), like vanilla recipes.
- **`/sanctum debug` missing when launched with `gradlew runClient`**: debug commands are now always enabled in the development environment, whatever the build type.

## [0.1.0-alpha.1] - Foundations: dimension, portal, sanctum lifecycle

### Added
- **Sanctum dimension** (`sanctumcraft:sanctum`): empty void with the End sky, no natural mob spawns, beds and respawn anchors disabled.
- **Rune Stone** block (8 per craft: 8 deepslate bricks + 1 amethyst shard) and **Sanctum Shard** item (4 amethyst shards + 1 ender pearl).
- **Sanctum portal**: build a Rune Stone rectangle (interior 2×3 to 21×21, corners optional) and right-click it with a Sanctum Shard. The shard is consumed. Breaking the frame breaks the portal.
- **Independent portals**: every portal has its own sanctum, timer and cooldown, saved with the world.
- **Sanctum lifecycle**:
  - entering a ready portal generates a new sanctum room by room while the player waits ("Generating… Room x / y")
  - up to 3 players (configurable) can join the same sanctum through the same portal, during generation or while it is open
  - 30-minute timer shown in a boss bar, warnings at 5 min, 1 min and a 10-second countdown
  - leave at any time through the exit portal of the entrance room (or `/sanctum leave`), come back while the timer runs
  - at zero, players still inside die and the sanctum is wiped over several ticks
  - the portal then recharges (30 min by default)
- **Room assembler**: 12 rooms on an 8×8 grid of 25×25 cells: random tree from the entrance + optional loops; destination = farthest room, nests = next far rooms; unused doorways are sealed.
- **6 placeholder rooms** (entrance, nest, destination, 3 halls), generated by `tools/gen_rooms.py`.
- **Protection**: survival players can't break or place blocks in the sanctum.
- **Safety**: sanctums still generating at shutdown are discarded; players reconnecting inside a collapsed sanctum die.
- Commands: `/sanctum status`, `/sanctum leave`, `/sanctum list` (OP), `/sanctum debug generate|close|closeall|time <s>|cooldown reset` (OP, debug build).
- Config file `config/sanctumcraft.properties`.
- FR / EN translations.
